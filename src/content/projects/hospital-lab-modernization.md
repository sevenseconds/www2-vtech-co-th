## Project Overview

We partnered with Metropolitan General Hospital to modernize their clinical laboratory, transitioning from manual processes to a fully integrated automated system.

## Key Deliverables

- Installation of automated chemistry and hematology analyzers
- Implementation of laboratory information system (LIS)
- Staff training and workflow optimization
- Quality control program development

## Results

The modernization reduced turnaround times by 40% and increased testing capacity by 60%, while maintaining exceptional accuracy and quality standards.

**Duration:** 6 months  
**Budget:** $2.5M  
**Team Size:** 15 specialists
