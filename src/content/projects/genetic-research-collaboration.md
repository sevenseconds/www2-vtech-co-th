## Research Goals

Identify genetic markers associated with common hereditary conditions in diverse populations.

## Our Contribution

- Next-generation sequencing services
- Bioinformatics analysis
- Data management and quality control
- Technical consultation

## Outcomes

The study analyzed 50,000 samples, identifying 15 novel genetic variants associated with cardiovascular disease risk. Results published in Nature Genetics.

**Duration:** 18 months  
**Samples Processed:** 50,000  
**Publications:** 3 peer-reviewed articles
