## Challenge

During the pandemic peak, our community needed expanded testing capacity to manage the COVID-19 outbreak effectively.

## Solution

We established a dedicated testing facility capable of processing 10,000 samples per day, with results available within 24 hours.

## Implementation

- Deployed automated RNA extraction systems
- Set up multiple PCR thermal cyclers
- Implemented 24/7 operations with rotating staff
- Integrated with state health reporting systems

## Impact

Processed over 500,000 tests with 99.8% accuracy, supporting public health decision-making and community safety.
