With a PhD in Microbiology from Stanford University, Dr. Chen has dedicated his career to understanding and combating infectious diseases. He has published over 50 peer-reviewed articles on antimicrobial resistance and rapid diagnostic methods.

Dr. Chen oversees our microbiology department, ensuring accurate identification of pathogens and timely susceptibility testing. His research initiatives keep our laboratory at the forefront of diagnostic microbiology.

**Expertise:**
- Clinical Microbiology
- Antimicrobial Resistance
- Rapid Diagnostic Methods
