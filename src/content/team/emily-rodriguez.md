Dr. Emily Rodriguez earned her PhD in Human Genetics from UC Berkeley and completed postdoctoral training at the National Institutes of Health. Her work focuses on translating genetic discoveries into clinical applications.

She directs our genetic testing programs, including carrier screening, hereditary cancer panels, and pharmacogenomic testing. Dr. Rodriguez also provides genetic counseling services to help patients understand complex genetic information.

**Focus Areas:**
- Molecular Genetics
- Pharmacogenomics
- Genetic Counseling
